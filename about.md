I'm Ihor Cherednichenko — an engineering leader and hands-on architect with 15+ years in software development, more than a decade of it spent leading multidisciplinary teams that build and run large, multi-tenant cloud platforms. These days a growing part of my work is figuring out where AI genuinely fits into the software development lifecycle — not as a demo, but as something a real team can rely on under production pressure.

This blog exists mostly out of curiosity. Agentic development is changing the *methodology* of building software faster than the industry can document it, and much of what gets written lands as either enthusiasm or fear. Having used these techniques daily in a large enterprise environment, I wanted a place to share what I can actually observe and measure: which practices hold up, which quietly fail, and why.

Most articles here grow out of [Quorum](https://github.com/ia64mail/quorum) — a research vehicle for multi-agent, semi-autonomous software development I built to study these questions in isolation, with an instrumented history and repeatable experiments instead of anecdotes.
